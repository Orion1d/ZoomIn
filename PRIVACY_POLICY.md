# Privacy Policy for ZoomIn Extension

**Last Updated: [Date]**

## Overview
ZoomIn ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles your information.

## Data Collection
ZoomIn does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing data, or search queries to our servers or any third-party services.

## What We Do
- The extension only operates on Google Search pages (google.com/search*)
- It enhances your search experience by replacing static map images with interactive Google Maps embeds
- All processing happens locally in your browser

## Permissions Explained
- **Storage**: Used only to save your preference for enabling/disabling the extension (stored locally in your browser)
- **Tabs**: Used only to send messages to the active tab when you toggle the extension on/off
- **Host Permissions (google.com)**: Required to inject the interactive map on Google Search pages

## Data Storage
- Your extension preference (enabled/disabled) is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's sync storage
- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking is performed

## Third-Party Services
- The extension embeds Google Maps directly from Google's servers
- We do not intercept or modify any data sent to Google Maps
- Your interaction with Google Maps is subject to Google's Privacy Policy

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store listing.
